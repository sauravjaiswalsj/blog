> 6-week structured revision plan targeting FAANG + IMC + Optiver + Goldman + Palantir. 2–4 hours per day.

<!-- truncate -->

> **Daily Rhythm (2–4 hrs):**
> 30 min — cold-solve 1 yesterday's problem · 90 min — solve 2–3 new problems · 30 min — read optimal solutions + write the pattern in your own words. Week 6: replace "new problems" with mock interview.
